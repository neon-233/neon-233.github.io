01 / ABOUT THE GROUP

About the Group

Understanding materials through simulation.
Exploring energy at the atomic scale.

The Yujin Ji Research Group is part of Prof. Youyong Li's group at the Institute of Functional Nano & Soft Materials (FUNSOM), Soochow University. We focus on the theoretical simulation and design of two-dimensional materials for energy storage and conversion. Through multiscale modeling, we investigate how surface atomic structures and morphologies evolve, uncover surface reaction mechanisms, and connect atomic and electronic structure with material performance. Combining these insights with databases of two-dimensional materials, we conduct high-throughput screening, material design, and performance optimization to guide the development of new energy materials.

03 / RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

Research

Understanding material properties, starting with atomic structure.

Learn more ↗
01

Structural Evolution

We use multiscale simulations to study how the surface atomic structure and morphology of two-dimensional materials evolve, identifying the principles behind these changes.

02

Surface Reaction Mechanisms

We investigate surface reactions on two-dimensional materials at the atomic and electronic scales, identifying key reaction steps and the factors that control them.

03

Structure–Property Relationships

We connect the microscopic structure of two-dimensional materials with their macroscopic properties, providing a theoretical basis for improving performance.

04

High-throughput Materials Screening

We combine databases of two-dimensional materials with high-throughput screening to identify promising systems for energy applications.

05

Materials Design & Optimization

We use theoretical insights to design and optimize two-dimensional energy materials for storage and conversion applications.
